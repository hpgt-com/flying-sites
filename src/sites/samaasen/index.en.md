The original site in the Harstad region, with flying since 1976. A small ridge north of the town that can be smooth as velvet, and turbulent in westerly wind.

## Launch

Launch right by the road at the top. Steep, short and not very even, so it requires wind in and is only used in ridge soaring conditions. Hence PP3.

## Landing

The landing is a bit messy. Do not land on the farmer's fields below the launch.
