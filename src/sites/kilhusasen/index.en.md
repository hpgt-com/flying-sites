Perhaps the club's very best site measured in flying hours. Easily accessible for everyone.

## Launch

Two launches. The lower one is the best and most used, but is 75 m lower than the top. The upper one is for days with little wind.

## Landing

Mainly on the airstrip. If you have to land in front of the hill, choose a spot where you are not in the way of people riding in the area, for example a bog or the old shooting range.
