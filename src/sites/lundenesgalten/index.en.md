Called Storgalten on the Kartverket map. Located on Grytøya just north of Harstad.

## Launch

The launch works for both PG and SPG.

## Landing

Both PG and SPG land on the field below Lundenesgalten. It is also possible to fly further down towards the parking and land in a suitable field or on a road, but check the landing first.
