One of the oldest flying sites, where people flew standard Rogallo wings. Strong, powerful thermals, and height gains of over 1000 m in ridge lift.

## Launch

Two launches: the lower one on the fields above Kasfjord, and the upper one on the top of Keipen.

## Landing

Landing on the field in Kasfjord, the same as the main landing for Nontua.
