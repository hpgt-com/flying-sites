Flystripa (the airstrip) at Åsegarden, on the Reinmoen military training area, is the club's site for winch towing and paramotor, and is also used for training in winch towing and paramotor. The club has an agreement with Forsvarsbygg for its use.

## Launch

Winch towing with an electric winch and paramotor take-off are done from the strip. This is only allowed under the agreement with Forsvarsbygg, and use must be notified to the Armed Forces at least two days in advance. Winch towing also requires a NOTAM. The main direction is into the wind along the strip, and the strip is used both ways: in easterly wind the winch launch starts from the opposite end. With a paramotor you can also take off in other directions in light wind. Watch out for the river and the forest on take-off.

## Landing

Landing on the strip. Paragliders are free to use the strip as a landing site, as long as you are sure you are not in the way of other activity in the area.
