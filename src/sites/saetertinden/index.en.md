High and beautiful, almost 1100 m asl, with many nice landings in Hårvika.

## Launch

Sætertinden is PP3 because it is a high-mountain launch. To the NE and E there are nice launches in winter, which in themselves are easy enough for PP2 and SPG2. In summer there are quite a few rocks there, and the launches are more technical. Several of the other launches are cliff launches (see Before you launch). In winter there are good launch spots in several places. In summer large parts of the top are covered in rocks, so study the map and meteogram before you hike up.

## Landing

Many nice landings in Hårvika.
