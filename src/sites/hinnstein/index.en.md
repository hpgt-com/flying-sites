Perhaps the club's best ridge-soaring site in southeasterly wind. Smooth, gets high, and has a superb view over the town.

## Launch

Good ground, but a bit uneven. The launch towards the east is short and rough.

## Landing

The main landing is the car park at Medkila school, which is also the alternative landing for Heia. The alternative landing is the football pitch at Medkila (Medkilabanen), which is the main landing for Heia.

The field below Rema 1000 Kilamyra is a larger alternative landing, about 2.6 km from launch. It makes Hinnstein more accessible for those who don't like the car park at Medkila. Most wings should reach it with a good margin.

- Pennants at several farms nearby show the wind.
- The sea on the east side gives a nice approach when landing towards the west.
- Lamp posts along the road make an approach towards the east demanding.
- The field slopes somewhat down towards the sea, from west to east.
- In southeasterly wind there can be rotor behind the forest west of the field.
