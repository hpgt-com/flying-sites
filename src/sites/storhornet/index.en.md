One of the club's most used and best sites. Covers many wind directions, has a large height difference and is an excellent starting point for cross-country flights.

## Launch

Three launches: upper, middle and lower. The main launch is marked on the map. Also used as a training hill. Open terrain with little mechanical turbulence, giving strong lift both in ridge lift and thermals.

## Landing

Landing by the sea. Speedriders can land by Bremnesvannet.

If you drive all the way up, you can also land at the upper parking. The field slopes downwards, so be careful if you fly in towards the north and the sea. Close by there is also a good bog to land on.
