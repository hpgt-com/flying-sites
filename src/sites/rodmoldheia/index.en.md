The number one thermal site in Harstad and Kvæfjord, and the first choice in southerly wind. Unproblematic for pilots of all levels, with great distance potential. The best season is May to mid-August.

## Launch

Large and fairly gentle launch area. The top landing is like a football pitch.

## Landing

Mainly on the bog right by the parking. PP2 and newly qualified pilots often use the landing below the launch.
