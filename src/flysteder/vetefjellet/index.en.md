On the way up towards Storlitinden, to the right of the peak. A good alternative when the wind veers from south towards east, with good options for top landing.

## Launch

The main direction is south, as on Storlitinden.

## Landing

Same landings as Storlitinden: at the Red Cross cabin on Kvæfjordeidet, or at the foot of the mountain. Good options for top landing.
