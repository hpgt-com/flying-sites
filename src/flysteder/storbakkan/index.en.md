A classic site with easy access and a short walk up, used both for training trips and long cross-country flights. Treat it with respect in strong wind.

## Launch

A grassy knoll by Storvatnet at Storjorda, between Sørvik and Straumen. A launch area with better ground than the lawn at home.

## Landing

Landing at the foot of the knoll.

In winter and spring Storbakkan is used as a training hill, and you then land on the ice on Storvatnet. Check that the ice is safe.
