One of the club's most used sites. People fly here all year round, for everything from short sled runs to thermals and nice XC flights.

## Launch

Large launch area on the top, with good ground in winter and somewhat rocky in summer. SPG mainly launch on the left side of the lift and land in a separate landing field in the forest (see «SPG landing» on the map).

## Landing

Large bog by the parking, so the landing and the car are in the same place.

SPG land in a separate landing field in the forest outside the ski area (grey target on the map). The slope itself is only flown when the ski centre gives permission.
