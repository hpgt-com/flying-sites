Perhaps the club's best ridge-soaring site in southeasterly wind. Smooth, gets high, and has a superb view over the town.

## Launch

Good ground, but a bit uneven. The launch towards the east is short and rough.

## Landing

The main landing is the car park at Medkila school, which is also the alternative landing for Heia. The alternative landing is the football pitch at Medkila (Medkilabanen), which is the main landing for Heia.
