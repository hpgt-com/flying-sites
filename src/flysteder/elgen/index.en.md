Far out by the open sea, with views towards Grytøya and the midnight sun. The mountain covers most wind directions, with separate launches for PG and SPG. Great for soaring and evening trips.

## Launch

The launches are around the east summit (506 m asl). The east side is often thermal in the morning and before noon in sunshine.

## Landing

When launching south, both PG and SPG land in the field below the SE launch, on the sea side of the road west of the westernmost house in Aun. Clear use with the neighbour, and before haymaking: land at the edge. The flagpole with a pennant on the farm shows the wind, and the neighbour likes to see people fly.

When launching north, you usually land in the field by the chapel at Elgsnes, where the car is parked. A power line on poles crosses the field, so check the landing before you fly. Occasionally people land on the foreshore at the far end of Elgsnes.
