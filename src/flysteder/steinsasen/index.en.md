One of the unknown gems, with a lovely launch area and one of the best top landing options. It takes little wind to soar in westerly wind.

## Launch

Two sides: west and southwest. The launch is partly a cliff and mountain launch, hence PP3.

## Landing

On the bog below the mountain.
