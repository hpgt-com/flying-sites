One of the most used sites in Kvæfjord, cleared right above Trastadsenteret. Especially in spring it can give intense flights with thermals to cloud base.

## Launch

The launch is easy to get up to. Flown only in ridge lift.

## Landing

Usually on the edge by the red boathouse, or on the road by the bus stop, which is more technical. If you land on the road, you must not obstruct traffic. Do not land on the field below the bus shelter. The landings along the sea are often in the lift zone, so it is easy to overshoot. Clear your landing with the club before you fly.
