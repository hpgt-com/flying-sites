Brilliant site in westerly winds, and the calmest of the three sites at Dale. Flying heights above 600 m asl are common.

## Launch

Two launches: the lower one at 96 m asl slightly to the left, and the upper one at 180 m asl over to the right. If there is little wind, it pays to walk to the upper one.

## Landing

In the field below the road, where you park and walk up to the launch. The landings along the road are in the lift zone.
