Superb spring thermals and cross-country flights of the very highest class, with thermals up to 1700–1900 m asl. Several distance flights from here.

## Launch

Launch area by the cairn. Somewhat rocky ground, so watch your lines.

## Landing

The main landing is the football pitch at Medkila (Medkilabanen). Alternatively the parking at Medkila school, which is also the landing for Hinnstein. Landing down by Medkilaosen is tight, so an inspection beforehand is recommended.
