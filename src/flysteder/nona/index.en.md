The highest peak on Grytøya north of Harstad, with a long hike up: around 1000 metres of ascent from the road.

## Launch

The best launch is towards W–SW, but it may potentially be possible to launch in several directions. The altitude means the site is probably also suitable for SPG, but this has not been tried yet.

## Landing

Landing down by the road, right next to the parking where the hike up starts.
