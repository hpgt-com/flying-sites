Can deliver powerful thermals on the right days.

## Launch

The main launch is at 540 m asl and is PP2. The walking route continues up to an alternative launch at 756 m asl, for SW, W and NW. It is PP3, since you cannot see the landing from there.

## Landing

The main landing is at the parking at Melåa. It is a bit technical, because low-voltage power lines and telephone lines on poles run in several places, so check the landing first. The alternative landing on the bog about 900 m south of the parking is very good (see the map).
