One of the classic sites, with one of the best ridges in southerly wind. The top is like a lawn, great both for launching and top landing.

## Launch

The launch is perfect, lawn everywhere.

## Landing

The main landing is right by the parking. Alternatively at Slettlia, but it can give turbulence in wind. Several bogs have also been used, including the large bog by Kilkamhuset, where large power lines mark the edge. Check them first because of high-voltage lines and cables in the air.
