Among the best thermal generators in the area, often with ridge lift at the same time. Rarely used, since Kilhusåsen offers an easier walk in NW. If you have walked up here, feel free to send the GPX track to the club.

## Launch

The launch is fairly steep, but cleared of bushes and scrub. Same level as Kilhusåsen, but requires reasonably good launch technique. Many fly here from Kilhusåsen.
