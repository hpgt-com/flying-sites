One of the favourite sites, with a view over Andfjorden and nice sled rides on summer evenings. Top landing everywhere.

## Launch

Good launch areas in all relevant directions, and top landing everywhere.

## Landing

Primarily on the field down in Kasfjord, by the sea. Alternatively by the tractor road that leads into the cabin area, and on the fields above Kaltdalen/Høgda.
