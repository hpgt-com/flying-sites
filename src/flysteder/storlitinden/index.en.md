One of the club's most used sites, with a short and easy walk up from Kvæfjordeidet. Ridge soaring, thermals and top-to-bottom flights.

## Launch

Launch areas on both sides of the cairn, on moss and grass.

## Landing

At the Red Cross cabin on Kvæfjordeidet, where the car is parked. There is also a landing at the foot of the mountain, which you see on the way up.
